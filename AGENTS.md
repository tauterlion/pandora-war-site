# Pandora player guide — maintenance instructions

Keep the site simple, static and easy to reference. Preserve the cinematic homepage and straightforward internal pages. No live scores, accounts or backend. Respect reduced-motion preferences, accessibility and mobile layouts. Do not invent server facts or release details; pending information stays marked pending. Do not expose the campaign seed, private configurations or exploration data. Keep optimized assets and do not upload full-resolution originals. Test changes on a preview branch before merging; never merge without the user's approval.
