End access · November 23

The End is locked during Days 1–2 and unlocks on Day 3 at midnight AST. Pandora reveals the approximate location of an in-border stronghold. How to find it ↗

Stronghold Discovery

On Day 3 (Monday, November 23), the path to the End opens. A stronghold within Pandora’s world border is available to explore and activate. Its location is revealed in-game, not on this website.

Day 3 · 12:00 AM ASTOne-time milestoneNo War Score
  1. Watch for the purple ACCESS UNLOCKED... action-bar message and a mysterious sound.
  2. About five seconds later, chat announces the stronghold’s approximate X and Z coordinates, rounded to the nearest 100 blocks. No Y-coordinate or waypoint is provided.
  3. Explore the surrounding area, locate the underground portal room and activate it with Eyes of Ender as usual.

Missed the announcement? From Day 3 onward, type /pandora stronghold in chat to retrieve the same approximate location. It remains available after reconnecting.

Important: Thrown Eyes of Ender still use vanilla targeting and may lead toward natural strongholds outside the world border. Use the announced search area instead. Eyes still work normally when inserted into portal frames.

Finding the stronghold early through legitimate exploration, looting it and activating its portal are allowed, but entering the End before Day 3 is blocked. Normal war and portal-trap rules apply. Stronghold Discovery does not award points or replace a competitive objective.

Player guide / Objectives

Objectives

Follow the event announcements and coordinates in-game. Secure objectives to earn War Score for your faction.

Supply DropControl PointRelic Extraction

Events require enough eligible players online: four for Supply Drop and Control Point, six for Relic Extraction, with at least two from each faction.

When a new objective is announced, its normal warning countdown lasts 7 minutes and 30 seconds. Use that time to prepare. Stronghold Discovery is a separate Day 3 milestone, not a scored objective. Stronghold Discovery ↗

Supply Drop

+3 War Score
5m descent30m claim window20s securing
  1. Travel to the announced coordinates. The crate takes five minutes to descend.
  2. Once it lands, you have 30 minutes to claim it. Interact with the crate and secure it for 20 seconds before the deadline.
  3. Only one player can secure the crate at a time. Defend your claimant or displace an enemy claimant.
  4. Collect the unlocked supplies. The successful faction earns 3 War Score once.

If nobody claims the crate within 30 minutes of landing, it disappears and no faction earns points or loot. A claim must finish before the deadline. No new objective is announced until the drop is claimed or safely expires.

Keep aiming at the crate, holding use and staying in range. Movement and damage alone do not interrupt securing, but knockback can.

The crate cannot be destroyed. Rewards improve as the campaign progresses.

Control Point

Up to +6 total
20m arrival window20m capture window6 scoring milestones
  1. Reach the marked capture zone within the 20-minute arrival window. The first eligible player to arrive starts a separate 20-minute capture window.
  2. Hold the zone with your faction. An enemy faction inside contests the point and pauses capture, but not the capture timer.
  3. Every scoring milestone captured awards 1 War Score. The six milestones are shared between factions. The event ends when all six are captured or the capture window expires.
1 player: 100%2: 110%3: 120%4+: 130%

Capture speed increases with teammates. A solo player needs 6 minutes of uncontested progress for all six milestones.

* Zone boundaries may not always align perfectly with the ground. Stay close to ground level and pay attention to the border lines.

Relic Extraction

+12 War Score
2m unlock5m extraction10s steal
  1. Reach the Relic and wait for it to unlock.
  2. Pick it up and escort the carrier to the extraction station. While carrying the Relic, you cannot attack, mine or interact with most objects.
  3. Deposit the Relic. The station location becomes public and extraction begins.
  4. Defend the station until its five-minute extraction progress completes. Enemies can steal control with an uninterrupted 10-second interaction.
  5. Each successful steal changes ownership and halves extraction progress. A completed extraction awards the controlling faction 12 War Score.
  • Q: Drop the Relic and regain your equipment.
  • Sneak + use: Teleport using an ender pearl from your inventory.
  • The extraction location is private to your faction until deposit.

After deposit, the battle can continue until extraction is completed. Damage alone does not interrupt a steal; breaking the interaction does.

* Raiding, theft and infrastructure destruction are permitted only when at least one other member of the targeted faction is online and actively playing (not AFK). This also applies to internal sabotage. Day 1 destruction must not intentionally kill players. An ongoing raid may be completed if defenders disconnect during it.

Portal fortifications are allowed, but traps designed to kill or immobilize arriving players before they can react are prohibited. Do not repeatedly kill players at their respawn locations; players who voluntarily return to combat may be attacked. Combat logging means intentionally disconnecting to escape combat, not genuine connection failures or crashes.

Faction conduct

Backstabbing and betrayal

Allowed. Stealing from teammates, destroying your own faction’s base and sharing legitimately obtained intelligence are forms of betrayal. Intentionally attacking or killing teammates, including through indirect means, is prohibited. All other server rules still apply.

Ceasefires and secret alliances

Allowed. Players and factions may negotiate, cooperate, form secret alliances and exchange legitimately obtained intelligence, including information gathered through reconnaissance. Official faction membership restrictions still apply.

Membership

You may leave and rejoin your original faction, but may never switch to the opposing faction. See faction membership ↗ for details.

Fair play

  • No cheating or exploits. Do not use cheats, unfair mods or bugs to gain an advantage.
  • No alternate accounts. Participate using your registered Minecraft account only.
  • Respect objective intentions. Play objectives as designed. Fighting, ambushes, TNT and fortifications are allowed. Disabling objectives, exploiting scoring bugs, duplicating rewards or manipulating attendance to exploit event activation is prohibited.
  • No deliberate server disruption. Do not build lag machines, spam entities or intentionally disrupt or crash the server. Legitimate battlefield destruction is allowed.
  • No unfair external intelligence. Do not use the world seed, external seed-mapping or tracking tools, replay recordings or administrative information to gain a competitive advantage. Reconnaissance, secret alliances, legitimate intelligence sharing, ordinary exploration and permitted JourneyMap features are allowed.
  • Keep the war within the game. Do not access another player’s account, impersonate administrators or harass players outside the game.

See Objectives ↗ for the intended mechanics.

Death policy

Keep all eligible equipment, regardless of quantity or inventory slot. Everything else follows normal death-drop behavior.

You keep

  • Armor and shields
  • Swords, axes, pickaxes, shovels and hoes
  • Bows and crossbows
  • Tridents, maces and spears

Durability, enchantments and item components are preserved.

You lose

  • Elytra, fishing rods and totems
  • Bundles with all contents
  • Food, potions and ammunition
  • Resources and building materials
  • Experience levels

Relic custody follows its own rules. Spears retain their vanilla combat mechanics. Selective retention is the approved launch policy; final release verification is pending.

Moderation

NatroGB is the sole administrator. Cheating, exploits and other violations are reviewed manually before disciplinary action. There are no automatic disciplinary kicks, bans or War Score deductions.

Report incidents with screenshots, timestamps or other evidence. Punishment depends on the circumstances and severity.

Troubleshooting

Modrinth fails to create or import the instance

Use a simple instance name such as Pandora. Do not use emojis or special characters such as ? when naming instances; they can cause folder or import errors. If the import failed, create a new instance with a simple name and try again.

Black world, but the HUD is visible

Press U to open RenderScale, disable FSR 1 and return render scale to 100%. This fixed the reported issue on a macOS client during testing.

World looks weird/distorted when I join

This is most likely an issue involving Distant Horizons and Iris Shaders. Press Delete to reload shaders or use K to toggle them off and back on. You can also do this through the shader settings.

In the current Pandora presets, BetterHitSounds is assigned to Insert. If a keybind has been changed or conflicts with Iris, open Options > Controls > Key Binds... and check the Iris and BetterHitSounds sections.

Pressing a key activates multiple functions

Go to Options > Controls > Key Binds... and look for conflicting keybinds. Assign a different key, or click an unwanted keybind and press Escape to unbind it. JourneyMap, Iris, BetterHitSounds and Simple Voice Chat may have overlapping bindings if you change their defaults.

The game runs poorly or has low FPS

Try disabling shaders with K (if enabled), lowering your render distance and reducing Distant Horizons' render distance in its settings. If needed, switch to the Performance edition. Import it as a separate instance rather than combining multiple editions.

Cannot join the server

If the server is available, try reconnecting a few times (3–5 times). Check your Minecraft username, whitelist status and that you are running the official distributed modpack. If the server is still being tested or is offline before launch, wait for the official opening announcement. Send the error message to NatroGB if the problem persists.

The server says my mods are incompatible

Check that you are running the latest official Pandora modpack. If you participated in testing, use a fresh instance for the official release rather than mixing old Pandora JARs or mod versions with the new pack.

Voice chat isn't working

Press V to open Simple Voice Chat. Check that your microphone is not muted, and select the correct microphone and output device in the voice chat settings. Follow the microphone setup if prompted. If voice chat still cannot connect, report the issue to NatroGB.

Faction voice groups are open: nearby players, including enemies, can overhear conversations. Voice recording is permitted, so keep that in mind when speaking.

Players are invisible

During multiplayer testing, disabling Very Many Players (VMP) restored player visibility. VMP has been excluded from the upcoming official modpack. Use the latest official edition rather than adding it back. If players are still invisible, report the issue to NatroGB, including when it happened and which players were affected.

Distant Horizons says its database is corrupted

Report the warning to NatroGB. Close Minecraft completely, open your Pandora instance folder in Modrinth, and locate Distant_Horizons_server_data. Move the affected server or dimension cache folder identified by the warning to a backup location outside the instance, including any accompanying SQLite WAL and SHM files. Reopen Minecraft and reconnect to rebuild the cache.

This is a temporary workaround, not a permanent fix. Distant terrain may take time to reappear. Do not delete your Minecraft saves or other instance files. If the warning returns, save your latest.log and report it before clearing the cache again.

Gameplay is lagging even though my FPS is fine

If movement or block interactions are delayed despite good FPS, it may be a connection or server issue rather than graphics performance. Report your ping if available, whether other players are affected, and what you were doing to NatroGB.

Death waypoint markers obstruct my view

Press J to open JourneyMap and look for the death waypoint option in its waypoint settings. Disable automatic death waypoints if you do not want them. You can also remove existing death waypoints individually from the waypoint manager.

Is this intentional?

These restrictions are part of Pandora's planned gameplay, not technical issues.

Why can't I enter the End?

The End is intentionally locked until Day 3. Activating a portal before then is allowed, but entering it is not. See Stronghold Discovery ↗ for how to locate Pandora’s portal.

Why are my Eyes of Ender pointing outside the world border?

Thrown Eyes of Ender retain their vanilla targeting and may lead to an inaccessible natural stronghold. From Day 3, use /pandora stronghold to retrieve the approximate coordinates of Pandora’s accessible stronghold. Eyes still activate the portal normally.

Why aren't events appearing?

Events are paused on Day 1 and require enough eligible players online during the war. See Objectives ↗ for requirements.

Why can't I see mobs or players on JourneyMap?

Entity radar is intentionally restricted to keep the campaign fair. This is not a graphics or minimap bug.

Why can't I break anything in the waiting room?

The waiting room is protected until the campaign is released. Once the countdown ends, you will enter the world and normal Day 1 gameplay begins.
